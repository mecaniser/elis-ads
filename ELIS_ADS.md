# Elis Ads

Elis Ads is a paid-media operating project for running, optimizing, and
auditing advertising across Google, Meta, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Microsoft,
Apple, Amazon, Reddit, Pinterest, Snapchat, and X.

## Operating focus

### Run

- Capture the business, account, KPI, privacy, and approval context.
- Build channel, campaign, budget, creative, tracking, and experiment plans.
- Prepare campaign launches as reviewable drafts.
- Require explicit approval, verification, an audit record, and rollback before
  any supported account change.

### Optimize

- Monitor pacing, delivery, conversion tracking, creative fatigue, policy
  status, and performance.
- Produce evidence-backed optimization drafts.
- Separate observed facts, assumptions, benchmarks, opportunities, and risks.
- Measure results over a declared evaluation window.

### Audit

- Review existing accounts and pre-launch campaign builds.
- Use deterministic controls and source-grounded findings.
- Keep health, evidence coverage, regulatory exposure, and opportunities
  separate.
- Render versioned JSON into consistent Markdown, HTML, or PDF reports.

## Safety position

Elis Ads is read-only by default. It does not permanently delete campaigns or
account objects. Live changes remain blocked unless the exact platform
capability and mutation plan pass the upstream approval, idempotency,
verification, audit, and rollback gates.
